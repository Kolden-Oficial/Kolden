## Who we are

Brayan's Finish is a Massachusetts-based finish carpentry and remodeling company. We specialize in the details that make a house feel finished — the clean lines of a baseboard, the perfect fit of a cabinet, the crisp edge of a trim run. It's the work most people notice only when it's wrong. We make sure it's right.

We're a hands-on, five-person team led by our owner, who is on every job site, on every walkthrough, and behind every quote. That's how we protect the standard our name is on.

---

## What we do

- **Finish carpentry** — baseboards, crown molding, casings and interior trim
- **Cabinets & built-ins** — kitchen cabinets, mudrooms, custom closets, storage solutions
- **Stairs** — treads, risers, railings and stair remodels
- **Painting** — interior finishing and touch-ups tied to our carpentry work
- **Vinyl installation** — flooring and finish surfaces
- **General remodeling** — full-scope residential remodels, coordinated end-to-end

---

## Who we work with

Two lanes:

- **Homeowners** in Boston, coastal Massachusetts, Rhode Island and New Hampshire looking for finish work that holds up — visually and structurally.
- **General contractors and builder contractors** who need a dependable finish crew that shows up, hits the spec, and moves the job forward on schedule.
