## Contexto — para o desenvolvedor da LP

Este FAQ endereça as **7 objeções canônicas** de homeowner considerando reforma high-ticket. Ordem intencional: começa pela dúvida operacional mais imediata (timeline), sobe para as objeções de confiança (licença, permits, warranty), depois trata a friction real do dia-a-dia (mudar de casa), fecha na oferta e no protocolo de imprevisto.

Cada resposta é ancorada em pelo menos um dos **3 diferenciais canônicos Vilela:**
- **Unmatched Cleanliness** — limpeza diária, home tratada como home
- **Transparent Communication** — updates claros, sem sumir mid-project, escopo escrito
- **Dedicated Craftsmanship** — atenção ao detalhe, punch-list, não corta atalhos

Voz: **honest, grounded, dedicated.** Sem AI slop ("elevate", "seamless", "unleash", "transform your life"). Frases curtas, informação concreta, e admissão do que é incerto quando cabe (Q4 sobre bathroom).

---

### Q1: How long does a typical kitchen or bathroom remodel take?

A full kitchen remodel usually takes 4–6 weeks, and a full bathroom takes 2–4 weeks — depending on scope, materials, and any custom fabrication. You get a firm timeline in your written estimate, and we stick to it. If something on our end causes a delay, you hear it from us first.

---

### Q2: Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Vilela Construction is a licensed and insured general contractor based in Massachusetts, serving Greater Boston and Southern New Hampshire. We carry general liability and workers' compensation coverage on every crew that steps into your home. You can ask for a copy of both certificates before we start — it's a normal request, and we're glad to send them.

---

### Q3: Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes. For any remodel that needs a permit — most kitchens and structural bathroom work do — we pull it, schedule inspections with your town or city, and coordinate everything from start to sign-off. You don't chase paperwork; that's on us. Your written estimate lists which permits are included in the price.

---

### Q4: Do we need to move out during the project?

Most homeowners stay in the house. For a kitchen remodel, we set up a temporary kitchen area with your fridge and microwave, seal off the work zone with plastic barriers, and clean up at the end of every workday. For a bathroom, if it's your only bathroom, we'll be straight with you about the day-by-day plan and options before demo day. We treat your home like our own — the point is to disrupt your life as little as possible.

---

### Q5: What's included in the free in-home estimate?

An in-home visit (usually 45–60 minutes), a walkthrough of your space and your vision, measurements, and a written scope with a real number — not a range. You leave the meeting knowing what's included, what's excluded, what the timeline looks like, and how payment is structured. No obligation, no pressure to sign anything on the spot.

---

### Q6: Do you offer a warranty on your work?

Yes. Every project ends with a punch-list walkthrough — you point out anything that isn't right, and we fix it before final payment. After that, we stand behind our craftsmanship: if something we installed fails from a workmanship issue, we come back and make it right. Material warranties (cabinets, appliances, tile) pass through from the manufacturer, and we'll walk you through each one during handoff.

---

### Q7: What's your process if we run into unexpected issues during demolition?

We stop, show you the finding, and quote the change in writing before we do anything about it. Rotted subfloor under old tile, hidden water damage behind drywall, outdated electrical that can't stay — those are the surprises demo can uncover. You get the finding, the fix options, and the cost in writing. No work continues on that item until you approve. No surprise invoice at the end.

## Confiança da entrega

**Forte** para Q1, Q2, Q3, Q5, Q6, Q7 — respostas falsificáveis (Thiago pode olhar cada uma e dizer "isso é verdade / isso não é", e o próprio homeowner pode auditar durante o processo).

**Testar** Q4 — a nuance sobre "if it's your only bathroom" depende do estilo de comunicação real do Bernardo/Thiago com o cliente. Se preferirem ser mais assertivos ("we recommend a temporary hotel stay if X"), ajustar.

**Ponto de atenção — Q3 (permits):** confirmar com Thiago se o preço do estimate SEMPRE inclui os permits ou se é linha separada. Se for separada, editar para *"the cost of any required permits is listed as its own line in your written estimate"*.

**Ponto de atenção — Q6 (warranty):** confirmar com Thiago o **prazo formal** da garantia de mão-de-obra (1 ano? 2 anos? lifetime?). Hoje a resposta diz "we stand behind our craftsmanship" sem prazo — está honest mas vago. Assim que Thiago der o número, editar para *"our workmanship warranty is X years"*.
