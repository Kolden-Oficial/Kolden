#### What Is a Deep Plane Facelift?

A deep plane facelift is a facial plastic surgery procedure that rejuvenates the midface, lower face, and neck. What sets this technique apart from a traditional facelift is that the surgeon accesses deeper facial structures—specifically, below the SMAS (superficial aponeurotic system), which is what a traditional facelift typically works with. A deep plane facelift also releases facial retaining ligaments, allowing the surgeon much greater freedom in repositioning the tissues of the face.
This often results in a profound, significant, and long-lasting facial rejuvenation. Repositioning the soft tissue of the face helps to restore youthful cheek contour. Lifting the cheek fat pads often obviates the need for fat grafting. Additionally, because the deeper facial layers are repositioned, there is minimal tension on the skin's surface, typically resulting in better incision healing.

#### What Are the Benefits of the Deep Plane Facelift?

Many experts and facial plastic surgeons agree that the deep plane facelift is a transformative procedure, allowing patients to look like the younger versions of themselves. Deep plane facelift benefits include:
\* Comprehensive facial rejuvenation, targeting jowls, sagging skin of the neck, and hollow appearance of the cheeks
\* Lifting the sagging facial tissue helps to achieve a more youthful facial contour, smooth out the wrinkles, and improve midface volume loss
\* A defined jawline
\* Minimal tension at incision sites, providing an opportunity for better healing
\* Natural-looking, long-lasting results

#### How Much Is a Deep Plane Facelift?

When performed by an expert in facial plastic surgery, the cost of a deep plane facelift can range from $30,000 to $100,000 or more. The final cost of a deep plane facelift depends on several variables, but it's one of the more expensive procedures due to the surgical expertise required.
While plastic surgery prices vary throughout the U.S., the true cost driver isn't simply geography. Major metropolitan areas like New York certainly have higher cost of living, but what matters most for pricing is that these desirable locations attract a concentration of highly specialized plastic surgeons with advanced expertise. It's this clustering of top-tier practitioners and their superior skill levels—not just the local cost of living—that primarily accounts for higher prices in these areas.

#### Deep Plane Facelift Cost Breakdown

This cost breakdown is intended to help patients understand exactly what they're paying for when they get a deep plane facelift.
**Surgeon's Fee:** The skill of your surgeon is one of the greatest determinants of the final cost of your facelift. It is worth it to seek out the expertise of a board-certified plastic surgeon who specializes exclusively in facial surgeries in order to have the safest experience and high-quality results. The deep plane facelift requires an in-depth understanding of facial anatomy and a high level of dexterity and artistry to execute the procedure properly. This is one of the main reasons it's one of the most expensive facelifts available.
**Anesthesia Fee:** Whether performed under general anesthesia or deep sedation, an experienced anesthesiologist is required for patient safety and comfort. Their fee and the price of the anesthesia are covered under this component of the total cost.
**Facility Fees:** This covers the cost of surgical staff, equipment, the physical space, and other related costs. An AAAASF-accredited surgical center ensures high-quality care, top-of-the-line equipment, and qualified staff.
**Pre- and Postoperative Care:** This includes consultations, follow-up appointments, medications, surgical garments, recovery supplies, and all those ancillary factors that make a procedure successful.

#### Is a Deep Plane Facelift Worth It?

The deep plane facelift has become the gold standard of facelifts for good reason: it is one of the most effective techniques for comprehensive facial rejuvenation performed today. Not only can it achieve profound yet elegant lifting and enhancement of the face, patients who undergo a deep plane facelift often do not pursue another facelift in their lifetime—meaning this is a one-time investment in lasting rejuvenation. These patients age gracefully and tend to look younger than their counterparts who did not undergo the procedure.
Aside from its impressive, long-lasting results, patients who have undergone a deep plane facelift report a profound increase in confidence and satisfaction with their appearance. Many find they look as youthful as they feel, allowing them to engage socially and professionally with renewed energy.
