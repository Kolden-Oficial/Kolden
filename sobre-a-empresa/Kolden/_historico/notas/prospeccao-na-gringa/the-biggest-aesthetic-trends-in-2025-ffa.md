The Biggest Aesthetic Trends in 2025

### The Biggest Aesthetic Trends in 2025

The beauty and cosmetics industry is constantly in flux, fueled by innovative treatments, evolving patient preferences, and shifting societal norms. From patients opting for nonsurgical procedures to more men seeking enhancements such as jaw filler, here are five of the biggest aesthetic trends in 2025.

#### 1. The Rise of Noninvasive Procedures

Nonsurgical procedures are having a moment — they offer incredible results without ever having to go under the knife. Because of this, minimally invasive treatments are often painless and typically involve less downtime than their surgical counterparts. This way, you can get back to your busy schedule almost immediately. These procedures are also quick, easy, and usually a fraction of the price.
Wrinkle relaxers like Botox® and Dysport®, which reduce the signs of aging, have been a firm favorite among beauty enthusiasts for a while now. In 2023, almost 9.5 million of these procedures were performed in the United States. As more people want to reverse the effects of time with just a few simple injections, this trend shows no signs of slowing down.
Thanks to the influence of social media and celebrity culture, our focus on achieving the perfect pout will continue. Filler is set to be another one of the biggest cosmetic trends, as this treatment can bring fullness and definition to your lips and balance any asymmetries.
Let's look at some other noninvasive procedures that will be trending throughout the year.

##### Nonsurgical Facelifts

One of the new aesthetic treatments of 2025 is the nonsurgical facelift. Also known as midface fillers or liquid facelifts, this treatment involves strategically injecting hyaluronic acid (HA) fillers like JUVÉDERM® and Restalyne® Lyft into areas such as your jawline, nasolabial folds, and cheeks. Say hello to a rejuvenated facial structure with more contoured cheekbones, firmer skin, and fewer wrinkles and fine lines.
The best part? The entire procedure only takes 20 to 35 minutes, with results that kick in immediately — depending on how much filler your practitioner uses, these results can last up to two years. While you may experience some mild swelling, this usually goes down after two to three days.

##### Nonsurgical Brazilian Butt Lift (BBL)

Many of us have dreamed of achieving a plump and more sculpted booty but feel the risks of a surgical BBL just aren't worth it. Enter the nonsurgical BBL, a much safer alternative if performed by experienced and licensed providers. Rather than surgically adding fat, this procedure works by injecting Sculptra® — a formula containing poly-L-lactic acid that helps your body naturally produce more collagen.
As the collagen gradually builds up over a couple of weeks to months, the results only get better over time. With some patience, you'll have fuller and bigger buttocks with reduced stretch marks and cellulite. The nonsurgical BBL can also help you smooth out hip dips. You can achieve all of this in around 30 to 45 minutes and enjoy a more lifted booty for up to five years.

##### Microneedling

If injectables aren't your thing, but you still want a youthful appearance, microneedling might be the cosmetic trend for you. Also known as collagen induction therapy, this treatment uses tiny needles to create punctures in your skin, stimulating a deep layer of the derma to kick-start collagen production rather than freezing or filling areas of your face.
As these needles promote new cell growth and a faster turnover, microneedling is the ultimate pick-me-up for your skin. Beyond helping to reduce wrinkles, it can also target acne scars, blemishes, hyperpigmentation, and stretch marks — who doesn't love a treatment that does it all?
The procedure takes about 30 minutes, and you'll likely see visible improvements within a week. To maintain your results, we recommend getting treatments every four to six weeks, with three to six treatments in total, depending on your goals.

##### RF Microneedling

Radiofrequency (RF) microneedling will become even more popular in 2025. It uses tiny needles to deliver radiofrequency energy into the skin, stimulating collagen production. RF microneedling can improve skin texture, tone, and firmness by treating fine lines, wrinkles, acne scars, stretch marks, and other skin conditions.
Like traditional microneedling, the procedure creates a controlled injury to trigger the skin's natural healing process. However, RF microneedling is considered the more advanced option that can target deeper skin issues and can provide faster, more noticeable results.

##### Laser Treatments

Laser treatments use concentrated light beams to treat skin concerns, addressing damaged skin cells while preserving healthy ones. Many patients use these treatments to reduce the appearance of wrinkles.
Laser treatments can also stimulate collagen production, improving skin texture and firmness while addressing vascular lesions, blemishes, and even conditions like eczema and psoriasis. You'll find a laser treatment for every beauty dilemma — from skin rejuvenation and fat reduction to hair removal.

#### 2. A Demand for Preventive Cosmetic Treatments

As mentioned, many people turn to cosmetic treatments like wrinkle relaxers to reverse the signs of aging. However, over the past couple of years, patients in their mid-to-late 20s and early 30s have started these procedures to delay aging. Preventive treatments will continue as one of the key 2025 beauty trends. For example, preventive Botox can help stop wrinkles in their tracks by injecting fine lines, which are essentially future wrinkles.
As much as it is a restorative anti-aging procedure, microneedling can also be preventive and keep wrinkles at bay. By encouraging collagen production, this treatment can help you hold onto your youthful glow for a little longer, keeping your skin plump and elastic.

#### 3. A Shift Toward Natural-Looking Results

The cosmetics industry has officially entered its “undetectable era.” Instead of dramatic transformations and pursuing perfection, there has been a shift toward a natural aesthetic that celebrates individual beauty. While most qualified surgeons and practitioners have always believed in the power of subtle enhancements, this value is finally reflected in patients' preferences.
In 2025, we're saying goodbye to a one-size-fits-all approach. Both surgical and nonsurgical procedures will be more personalized than ever before, helping you look like the best version of yourself. For example, recent innovations in implant shapes, materials, and sizes will allow you to achieve a much more discreet outcome with breast augmentation surgery. Injectable treatments will also focus on creating balance and harmony.
When you head to a reliable medspa, the experts will help guide you with what they think is best for your facial features and body type.

#### 4. An Increase in Treatments Restoring Facial Volume

Thanks to prescription weight management medications such as Semaglutide, many Americans have been empowered to take control of their health journey. In fact, around two in five adults who use GLP-1 drugs do so to lose weight. While weight loss is often the first step toward living a happier and healthier life, some people might notice that their faces look a little less full. Their skin may also feel less firm.
Because of these concerns, one of the major cosmetic trends in 2025 will be procedures that restore facial volume and target skin laxity. Most notably, facial fillers are about to take off. Also known as dermal fillers, this treatment can help you achieve that snatched and youthful look by reconstructing your face's natural contour and bringing back its volume. Plus, it tightens the skin and reduces wrinkles for a smoother appearance.

#### 5. More Male Aesthetic Procedures

Although cosmetic procedures are often associated with female patients, 2025 will see more men embrace aesthetic enhancements. Society is finally breaking down the stigma only women care about their appearance, making it increasingly normalized and accepted for men to show an interest in surgical and nonsurgical treatments. Male patients are loving the following procedures:
\* Jawline filler
\* Botox
\* RF Microneedling
\* Lip filler
\* Laser Services
Beyond helping to give maturing skin a little boost, Botox and filler can also be tailored to preserve and strengthen more masculine features. For example, jawline contouring can give you a more chiseled jaw, allowing you to have features that make you feel good in your skin.
