### Eden AI

Eden AI provides a unified API to access, compare, and manage multiple AI models from various providers. Integrating Eden AI with Make.com enables users to automate tasks like text analysis, translation, or image recognition using diverse AI engines in their workflows.

### Requirements

To use the Eden AI app in Make, you must have an active Eden AI account and an API key, create your account on Eden AI. For subscription, refer to the Eden AI pricing page.

### Connect Eden AI and Make

To get started, you must first create a connection between Eden AI and Make , allowing the two services to communicate.
1
**Obtain your API Key in your Eden AI account**
2
**Create the connection in Make.**

#### Obtain your API Key in your Eden AI account

Before you create the connection in Make , you should first obtain your **API Key** in your Eden AI account.
To obtain your **API Key** :
1
Navigate to the **homepage** after logging into your account.
2
Copy the **API key** displayed on the **homepage** .
You will use this value while creating a connection in Make .
Because the UI might have changed, the steps you see on your screen might be different, check the vendor documentation for updates.

#### Create the connection in Make

1
Log in in your Make account, add the Eden AI module to your scenario , and click **Create a connection.**
If you add a module with an instant tag, click **Create a webhook** , then **Create a connection.**
2
*Optional:* In the **Connection name** field, enter a name for the connection.
3
Enter the **authentication credentials** you copied from your Eden AI account or follow the on-screen instructions to create the connection.
For detailed information on connecting an application, see Connect an application page.
