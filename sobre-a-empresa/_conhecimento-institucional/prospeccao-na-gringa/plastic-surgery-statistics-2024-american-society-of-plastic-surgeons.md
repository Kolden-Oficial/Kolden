### Plastic Surgery Statistics 2024

Plastic surgery procedural statistics from the American Society of Plastic Surgeons.

###### A Message from

##### Scott Hollenbeck, MD

###### Immediate-Past President of the American Society of Plastic Surgeons

These ambiguous economic times defined one clear trend: Patients continue to value and invest in plastic surgery procedures and treatments. Despite economic uncertainty, demand has remained steady in 2024, showing us more and more patients are prioritizing their aesthetic health because it contributes to an overall better mental mindset and physical health. No matter the economy, people want to look and feel their best so they feel prepared to face the challenges of everyday life.
Plastic surgery goes far beyond aesthetics. It also includes reconstructive surgeries that span every age group, from infants to seniors. In 2024, this category saw the largest growth, with a 2% increase. Trauma, illness and birth defects often leave behind visible and invisible scars that can impact patients' long-term quality of life and mental well-being. Plastic surgeons are key members of care teams who guide the healing process since they specialize in repairing complex structures, helping patients heal physically and emotionally. The data suggests reconstructive work remains in high demand.
Minimally invasive treatments remain popular, both as part of self-care regimens and as an affordable luxury. Injectables, including neuromodulators such as Botulinum toxin injections, commonly known as Botox, fillers and lip augmentation, reign as favorites because they offer little downtime, few side effects and almost immediate results at an affordable price point.
For the first time, we measured prescription weight loss medications. These drugs help people live healthier lives, but are also reshaping their bodies and features. Plastic surgeons are a critical component in many people's weight loss journey because they rejuvenate and contour faces and bodies. This allows people to feel at ease and confident in their new bodies.
Plastic surgeons have always led the medical field in cutting-edge advancements, yet patient safety and education remain our top priorities. Board-certified plastic surgeons remain the gold standard in care by providing safe, effective treatment, both reconstructive and aesthetic. ASPS Member Surgeons are worth the investment because we empower patients by supporting their whole health and informed decision-making.
This report shows us where plastic surgery was in 2024 and provides glimpses into its future. Each number represents a real person who was transformed on the outside, and most likely the inside as well, by the skilled hands of a plastic surgeon who helps restore confidence, function or well-being. This data not only helps to create informed conversations around the intricacies of our profession but also highlights the commitment of ASPS Member Surgeons to excellence, innovation and, above all, patient safety.
I sincerely thank all ASPS Member Surgeons who contributed to this report with both data and expert insights. I am also grateful to everyone who designed and published this report to make it accessible to both plastic surgeons and the wide range of people who will use it for a variety of purposes.
This report does not close the book on plastic surgery. Instead, the data opens the dialogue for us to start writing the next chapter to shape the future of the specialty, ensuring that we always prioritize the people behind the numbers.
Sincerely,

#### Overall Trends

#### Face and Neck

#### Face and Neck

Patients sought subtle, natural-looking, undetectable results that enhanced their unique features. Neck lifts and nose reshaping (rhinoplasty) saw the most growth at 2%. Facial procedures were popular overall, possibly driven by the use of semaglutide weight loss drugs, such as Ozempic, which can cause skin laxity around the face.

#### Chest and Core

#### Chest and Core

Body contouring procedures remained popular, possibly because more individuals were using weight loss medications, which can cause loose, sagging skin. Buttock lifts saw the most growth at 3%. Tummy tucks continued to be a favorite procedure to help weight loss patients focus on sagging skin that can't be addressed with diet and exercise.

#### Lower Body and Extremities

#### Lower Body and Extremities

Post-weight loss body reshaping grew in 2024. Arm lifts and thigh lifts rose 2% and 3%, respectively, as patients sought to address excess skin after using semaglutide weight loss medications. These procedures allowed patients to have a sculpted, harmonious look to complete their weight loss journey.

#### Demographic Data

#### Gender

##### Women

Liposuction was the most popular procedure for women in 2024, while tummy tucks were also a favorite. These two remained go-to procedures, particularly after pregnancy or weight loss, as women sought to restore their silhouettes. Breast augmentation also was popular, but the trend shifted towards smaller, more proportionate implants. Meanwhile, breast lifts saw an increase, emphasizing the focus on a perkier bust.

##### Men

Men embraced more cosmetic enhancements in 2024. Ggynecomastia surgery (male breast reduction) was the most popular procedure, as men sought to define their chest contours. Liposuction and tummy tucks were also favorites to tone and tighten the core. Men wanted natural-looking results to boost their confidence in both their personal and professional lives.

#### Ages

##### Ages 19 and Under

Procedures for this age group are always approached with caution and parental consent. Note that some procedures in this category were only for patients 18 and older. Ear surgery saw the most growth in 2024, followed by breast reduction for both men and women. Most procedures in this demographic focus on helping young patients address long-standing concerns and increase self-esteem.

##### Ages 20-29

Gen Z explored aesthetics in 2024 with a focus on confidence and individuality. Facial and breast procedures grew, including chin augmentation, nose reshaping and male breast reduction. Neuromodulator injections, such as Botox, were the most popular minimally invasive treatment for this age group, as Gen Z embraced preventative skin care and subtle tweaks.

##### Ages 30-39

Millennials embraced breast procedures in 2024. Breast augmentation and male breast reduction saw the most growth in this demographic. This age group wanted procedures that delivered balanced, natural-looking results. They favored neuromodulator injections as the most popular minimally invasive treatment.

##### Ages 40-54

Gen X drove the majority of cosmetic procedures in 2024. Lift procedures focused on the buttocks, lower body and thighs grew the most with this demographic. Minimally invasive neuromodulator injections dominated for this group. Many merged a combination of surgical and minimally invasive treatments for comprehensive rejuvenation.

##### Ages 55-69

Facial procedures including face, neck and forehead lifts grew for Baby Boomers in 2024. Neuromodulator injections were the favored minimally invasive treatment. This demographic signaled a strong demand for classic, long-lasting solutions to facial aging that offered refined, natural results for a refreshed look.

##### Ages 70 and Over

Patients over 70 increasingly pursued plastic surgery as a way to reflect their vitality. Facial procedures, including face, neck, forehead and eye lifts, as well as facial fat grafting, each grew in this demographic. This demographic's favorite minimally invasive treatment was neuromodulator injections. This age group sought results that matched their active lifestyle and youthful mindset.

#### Regions

##### South Atlantic

Approximately 437,800 procedures were performed in this region in 2024, representing 28% of all cosmetic procedures in the United States. The largest volume of procedures was Brazilian butt lifts (50%), followed by liposuction (35%). Neuromodulation injections (30%) and lip augmentation with injectables (29%) had the largest volume of minimally invasive procedures.

##### South Central

Approximately 288,600 procedures were performed in this region in 2024, representing 18% of all cosmetic procedures in the United States. The largest volume of procedures was butt implants (43%), followed by lower body lifts (27%). Skin resurfacing (24%) had the largest volume of minimally invasive procedures.

##### Mountain & Pacific

Approximately 460,400 procedures were performed in this region in 2023, accounting for 29% of all cosmetic procedures in the United States. The largest volume of procedures was cheek implants (74%), followed by buccal fat pad removal (54%). Noninvasive skin tightening (44%) had the largest volume of minimally invasive procedures.

##### New England & Middle Atlantic

Approximately 173,900 procedures were performed in this region in 2024, representing 11% of all cosmetic procedures in the United States. The largest volume of procedures was butt lifts (27%), followed by a tie for chin liposuction (17%) and nose reshaping (17%). Noninvasive fat reduction (17%), lip augmentation (17%) and hyaluronic acid fillers (17%) tied for the largest volume of minimally invasive procedures.

##### North Central

Approximately 224,900 procedures were performed in this region in 2024, representing 14% of all cosmetic procedures in the United States. The largest volume of procedures was chin augmentation (33%), followed by male breast reduction (19%). Spider vein treatment (33%) had the largest volume of minimally invasive procedures.

#### Methodology

###### Methodology

The annual questionnaire was distributed to over 5,000 board-certified plastic surgeons who are ASPS members. We received almost 1,000 responses, which were included in the final sample.

###### Interpretation

To give the data added breadth and to provide a more comprehensive report, where indicated, ASPS combined survey responses with procedural data from CosmetAssure.

###### Validity

The results of the survey are based on a 95 percent confidence level with a ±4.62 percent margin of error.

#### Partner

ASPS is dedicated to bringing you the most accurate and reliable data in the specialty. For 2024, the Society used data from member surgeons and from an affiliated company – CosmetAssure. Combined, these sources give the data added breadth and depth to provide a more comprehensive report, strengthening credibility as a resource for doctors, patients, researchers and the media.
