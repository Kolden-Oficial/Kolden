### Top 10 Most Popular Plastic Surgery Procedures in 2025

```
*  January 13, 2026
*  By Dr. Michael J. Brown
```

Plastic surgery has become more refined, accessible, and innovative than ever before. Whether for aesthetic enhancement or reconstructive purposes, millions of people are turning to surgical and non-surgical procedures to look and feel their best.
Here were the **Top 10 Most Popular Plastic Surgery Procedures** in 2025:

#### **1. Rhinoplasty (Nose Surgery)**

Rhinoplasty remains one of the most requested procedures worldwide. It reshapes the nose to improve appearance or correct breathing issues. With modern techniques, results are more natural-looking than ever. Different parts of the nose both inside and outside can be improved upon.
**Popular Among** : Men & women aged 18–45
**Average Recovery** : 1 week until back at work, 3 weeks to exercise

#### **2. Liposuction**

Liposuction targets stubborn fat deposits that are resistant to diet and exercise. A small metal tube is passed into the fatty areas and then the areas are reduced by either heating or mechanically bursting the fat cells and then sucking out the fat to improve contouring. It is not a weight reduction surgery.
**Popular Areas** : Abdomen, flanks, thighs and neck.
**Average Recovery** : Out of work for 1 or 2 days, wearing a garment for a few weeks. Exercise 2 weeks.

#### **3. Breast Augmentation**

Still a top choice, breast augmentation enhances breast size and shape using implants or fat transfer. Customization options have improved dramatically in recent years.
**Popular Among** : Women aged 20–60
**Implant Options** : Saline, silicone, gummy bear cohesive breast implants. Above and below the muscle.

#### **4. Facelift (Rhytidectomy)**

A facelift reduces visible signs of aging by tightening skin and underlying tissues. Modern facelifts are subtle and often paired with eyelid surgery or fat grafting.
**Typical Results** : 10–15 years younger appearance
**Average Recovery** : Presentable in public by 7-10 days, 2–3 weeks before exercise.

#### **5. Tummy Tuck (Abdominoplasty)**

Perfect for post-pregnancy or weight loss, a tummy tuck removes excess skin and tightens abdominal muscles. Often combined with liposuction.
**Ideal For** : Postpartum or post-weight loss patients
**Downtime** : Return to work 7-10 days, garment for a few weeks, no exercise for at least 4 weeks.

#### **6. Blepharoplasty (Eyelid Surgery)**

This procedure revises drooping eyelids and under-eye bags, restoring a more youthful and alert appearance.
**Upper or Lower Eyelids**
**Downtime** : 7–10 days depending on surgery and degree of bruising.

#### **7. Brazilian Butt Lift (BBL)**

A BBL uses fat transfer to enhance the shape and size of the buttocks. The results are more natural than butt implants and it also contours other areas of the body in the process.
**Procedure Type** : Fat transfer from abdomen/thighs
**Recovery** : 2–3 weeks (no sitting for ~4 weeks)

#### **8. Botox and Dermal Fillers (Non-Surgical)**

While technically non-surgical, injectables dominate the cosmetic scene. Botox like products elax the underlying muscles which aids to smooth fine lines, while fillers restore volume to cheeks, lips, and more.
**Results** : Immediate to 2 weeks
**Downtime** : Minimal

#### **9. Chin & Jawline Contouring**

Surgical or injectable contouring of the chin and jawline is increasingly popular among both men and women looking for a more defined profile.

#### **10. Hair Transplant Surgery**

Especially popular among men, modern hair transplants offer natural, long-lasting results with minimal scarring thanks to FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) techniques.
**Techniques** : FUE or FUT
**Results** : Noticeable in 6–12 months

##### **Final Thoughts**

Plastic surgery is no longer just about aesthetics—it's about confidence, balance, and self-care. Whether you're considering a minor touch-up or a more transformative procedure, always consult with a **board-certified plastic surgeon** to ensure safety and optimal results. There is are many differences between a surgeon and a nurse.
